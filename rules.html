How To Play

Welcome to the Spelling Bee Game!

1. This game has 3 difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard. Each level contains 10 questions.
2. After you choose a level, the game will start automatically and the timer will begin running. Each game session has a time limit of 10 minutes.
4. During the game, you will see these buttons:
  • Play Audio : Plays the word that you need to guess.
  • Hint : Gives an additional clue for the word.
  • Check : Submits your answer and shows whether your answer is correct or incorrect.
5. Scoring Rules:
  • Easy → Correct Answer = +20 points | Incorrect Answer = 0 points
  • Medium → Correct Answer = +20 points | Incorrect Answer = -5 points
  • Hard → Correct Answer = +20 points | Incorrect Answer = -10 points
6. Make sure to spell each word correctly before submitting your answer. When the timer runs out, the game will end and you cannot continue playing.
7. You can listen to the audio as many times as needed. Try to get the highest score before the time runs out.
